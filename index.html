About

I'm an undergraduate researcher working at the intersection of robotics, computer vision, and machine learning. Most of my work is about closing the gap between a learned model and a robot operating in the real world: navigation in unknown environments, vision-based UAV control, and planners that account for strict temporal constraints.

At AeroMIT I lead the autonomous drone research subsystem, where I architect work on UAV perception, navigation, and control for challenging, GPS-denied environments. I develop ROS 2 / MAVLink workflows and Gazebo simulations for perception-driven autonomy.

At the HIRO Lab, IISc Bengaluru, I developed a Model-Based Diffusion planner for a non-holonomic robot. Differentiable Signal Temporal Logic objectives guided obstacle safety, goal reaching, and time-constrained waypoints across offline and online receding-horizon planning architectures.

I care about measurable systems: cross-validation across unseen rockets, consistent robot maps, and control loops that meet their timing targets. My first-author work at IC3 2026 reports physics-informed flight-phase classification from GPS-derived telemetry.

Sounding Rocket Flight-Phase Classification

code ↗

Physics-informed ML · Basis of the IC3 2026 paper · evaluated on seven rockets

  • Classifies flight phase — boost, coast, apogee, descent, landed — from GPS telemetry alone, with no barometric pressure sensor available.
  • PI-XGB: a physics-penalized objective that reached 0.838 ± 0.112 mean macro-F1 versus 0.830 for baseline XGBoost under Leave-One-Rocket-Out cross-validation.
  • PhaseNet: multi-scale temporal convolutions, attention fusion, and physics-informed losses, with streaming inference and early apogee prediction.
